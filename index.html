Meet your comedic mediums

"Why did the chicken go to a Seyoncé show?”
“To talk to the Other Side.”

SEYONCÉ makes unscripted comedy with the audience, not at them. A suggestion becomes a scene; a scene becomes a strange little world that exists for one night only.

We are your premier comedic mediums, conjuring razor-sharp wit and unscripted brilliance out of thin air. Your suggestions become scenes, and every performance takes a different turn. No scripts, no two shows alike—just a room full of people making something wonderfully unexpected together.
